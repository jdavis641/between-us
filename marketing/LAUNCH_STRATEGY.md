# Launch Testing & Optimization (10/12/26 to 01/13/27)

## Overview
The application will launch the week of 10/12/26. The goal of this phase is to strictly evaluate the **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** and optimize "new subscriber" volume. 

## The Ad Flywheel
1. **Organic Seeding**: Deploy the initial batches of short-form video utilizing the **AIDA framework** (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action). Focus heavily on emotional hooks from both masculine and feminine perspectives.
2. **Data Evaluation**: Identify which videos drive the highest volume of new subscribers for the lowest cost per view/engagement.
3. **Reinvestment (The Flywheel)**: Reinvest the recurring revenue generated by the initial subscriber base directly into Meta and TikTok ads to boost the best-performing organic content.

## Campaign Hooks (AIDA Format)
*To be generated by the CMO Agent via \`npm run marketing:launch\`.*
- **Masculine Perspective**: "Understanding Her Desires"
- **Feminine Perspective**: "Communicating Your Boundaries Safely"
- **Couple Perspective**: "Rekindling the Spark"

# Valentine's Day 2027 Campaign (Target: 2/14/27)

## Strategy
Position the app as the ultimate tool to safely explore desires and enhance relationship connections.

## The Rollout
- **Start Date**: 1/14/27 (exactly one month out).
- **Automation**: A dedicated GitHub Actions cron workflow (`cmo-valentines-rollout.yml`) triggers the CMO Agent on 1/14/27 to generate a 7-day bulk faceless marketing blitz.
- **Content**: Cross-platform daily auto-posting to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- **Conversion Mechanism**: Drive high-intent holiday traffic directly to the web-only PWA landing page.
