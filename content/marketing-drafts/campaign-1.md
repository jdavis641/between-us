# AIDA Marketing Campaign Draft

## Hook 1: Masculine Perspective (TikTok/Reels)
**Attention:** You ever feel like date night has just become... Netflix and falling asleep?
**Interest:** We all hit that routine. But what if there was a way to completely reset the tension between you two, without having to plan a massive elaborate date?
**Desire:** Imagine having an AI Game Master that learns exactly what you both crave—and safely guides you into new scenarios, ensuring she feels fully secure and you both get exactly what you want.
**Action:** Try the "Between Us" weekend roleplay engine. Spark the tension back up tonight.

## Hook 2: Feminine Perspective (Instagram)
**Attention:** Do you wish your partner would take the lead in the bedroom, but without crossing any boundaries?
**Interest:** It's hard to communicate those fantasies without feeling awkward or vulnerable.
**Desire:** "Between Us" uses an encrypted AI to find the safe intersection of your desires. It gives him the exact blueprint and secret instructions to sweep you off your feet, entirely within your comfort zone.
**Action:** Link your profiles today and let the AI plan your next unforgettable weekend.
